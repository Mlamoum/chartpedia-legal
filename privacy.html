Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Chartpedia (@ChartpediaOfficial) publishes data visualisation videos to social platforms. This policy explains what we do and do not do with personal information.

The short version: we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. We have no user accounts, no sign-ups, no newsletter, no cookies and no analytics on these pages. We use platform APIs only to publish our own videos to our own channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information from you.

Chartpedia has no login, no registration, no contact form and no shopping facility. These policy pages are static documents; they set no cookies, run no trackers and log no visitor analytics.

The only way we encounter information about you is through ordinary public engagement with our content on a third-party platform — for example, a public comment, like, follow or share. That information is collected and held by the platform, under the platform's own privacy policy, not by us. We see it only in the same aggregate or public form any other viewer would.

If you email us, we hold your message and address only for as long as it takes to reply to you.

3. Use of Platform APIs

Chartpedia uses the developer APIs of the platforms it publishes on (for example the YouTube Data API, and TikTok's content posting API) for a single, narrow purpose: to upload and publish our own videos to our own channels automatically, together with their titles, descriptions and tags.

Specifically, we confirm that we do not use these APIs to:

  • read, collect, store or process any other user's personal data;
  • access private or non-public information belonging to any user;
  • message, follow, or interact with users on your behalf;
  • build audience profiles, or sell or share any data with third parties;
  • authenticate or act on behalf of anyone other than the operator of the Chartpedia channels.

API credentials authorise our own channel accounts only, and are stored privately on our own systems. They are never shared.

4. Data Retention

We retain no personal data, because we collect none. There is no user database to retain, export or delete.

What we do keep are our own production materials: scripts, data sources, generated imagery and rendered videos. These contain no personal information about viewers.

Email correspondence is deleted once it is no longer needed. If you have emailed us and would like your message removed sooner, ask and we will delete it.

5. Third-Party Services

Our content is distributed through third-party platforms, and our production process uses third-party tools. When you watch our videos, you are on their service, and their privacy policy governs what they collect — typically including device information, viewing history and engagement.

This applies to, among others:

  • YouTube (Google) — where our videos are hosted and watched;
  • TikTok — where our short-form content is published;
  • GitHub Pages — which hosts these two policy pages and may log requests as part of ordinary web hosting.

We have no control over, and accept no responsibility for, the data practices of these platforms. Please review their policies directly. Links from our content to external sites are provided for reference only.

6. Children

Chartpedia content is general-audience educational material and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

7. Your Rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is normally nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. If you believe we hold something about you, contact us and we will confirm and, where applicable, delete it. Requests about data held by YouTube or TikTok must be made to those platforms.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
